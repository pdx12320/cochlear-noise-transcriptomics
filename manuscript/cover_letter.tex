Dear Editor,

Please consider ``Cellular organization of inflammatory transcription in the mouse cochlea after acoustic trauma'' for publication in \textit{Scientific Reports}.

The study examines inflammatory transcription across stromal, vascular and immune populations in the mouse cochlea using three public transcriptomic datasets. Fibrocyte-associated TNF--NF-\(\kappa\)B enrichment coexists with opposing endothelial and myeloid responses, while endothelial proteasome-associated transcription connects the cellular response to whole-cochlea protein-quality-control programs.

The contribution is the combination of animal-level pathway estimates with leave-one-animal-out and annotation-sensitivity analyses. The principal enrichment directions persist after individual-animal omission, and recurrent gene-localization patterns are retained under the alternative annotation. These results place whole-tissue inflammatory signals in their cellular context.

The manuscript is accompanied by Supplementary Information and a public archive containing the analysis scripts, source data, model objects and validation records. The archive is available at \url{https://github.com/pdx12320/cochlear-noise-transcriptomics}.
